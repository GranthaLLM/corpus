- EN: Com.—Then, i.e., after a year, Kabandhi having approached Katyayana asked ‘Oh, worthy master, whence do these creatures, Brahmins and the rest, arise?’ This question is intended to elicit what results are attained and what path is gone through, by combining Aparavidya and Karma.
